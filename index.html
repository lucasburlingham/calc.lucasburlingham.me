All calculations assume a spherical Earth (R = 6,371 km). Distances are geometric approximations. Fresnel zone radius is computed at the path midpoint for the 1st zone (r₁ = √(λD/4)); D uses the optional observer-target distance when provided, otherwise max geometric line-of-sight. 60% of r₁ is the minimum recommended obstacle clearance for reliable radio links.
